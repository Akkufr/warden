LEGAL SPECIFICATION
01 Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India)
  • Section 4 & 6
    Explicit, Informed Consent: Biometric facial data is processed strictly with unambiguous, affirmative consent from the data principal for the specified fraud prevention purpose.
  • Section 8(7)
    Zero Raw Media Retention: Personal data must be erased as soon as the specified purpose is fulfilled. Raw photos/video streams are discarded immediately from memory post-inference; only one-way vector embeddings are preserved.
  • Section 12 & 13
    Right to Withdraw & Erasure: Data principals have an absolute right to revoke consent at any time. Revocation triggers instant and permanent purging of vector records from the active FAISS index.
02 EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — Article 9
  • Article 9(1) & (2)(a)
    Special Category Biometric Processing: Processing of biometric data for uniquely identifying natural persons is strictly prohibited unless explicit, voluntary, and revocable consent is obtained.
  • Article 17
    Right to be Forgotten: Immediate mathematical and cryptographic deletion of biometric embeddings across all persistent stores upon withdrawal of consent.

STAGE 01 < 3.0 ms
Resolution-Tiered Preprocessing DYNAMIC DOWNSCALE
Dynamic scale capping to 640p/720p maintains deterministic sub-30ms execution budgets regardless of input source resolution (4K, 1080p, or 720p).
STAGE 02 TEMPORAL
Adaptive Temporal Sampling 2.5 FPS / SCENE CUT
Intelligent frame stride decimation samples video content at 2.5 frames/sec, avoiding redundant frame extraction while guaranteeing full sequence coverage.
STAGE 03 ~ 12.5 ms
Per-Frame Face Detection (YuNet) 5-PT LANDMARKS
Stateless neural face detection with OpenCV DNN YuNet extracts 5 key facial coordinates (eyes, nose, mouth corners) with robust handling of occlusions and cuts.
STAGE 04 < 0.5 ms
Intra-Content Identity Deduplication 82% QUERY REDUCTION

STAGE 05 ASYNC

STAGE 06 < 1.8 ms

Sub-linear cosine similarity search across in-memory L2-normalized SFace feature representations with calibrated 50% decision boundary.
